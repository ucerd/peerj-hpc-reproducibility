# CAID dashboard — evidence provenance and interpretation

Reporting window: 2023 through 22 November 2026 (management-reported; exact project dates not independently audited). Source: management statement on 22 November 2026 and author-supplied internal `CAID_Dashboard_2026_Dec..(2).xlsx`. The source filename refers to December; no post-cutoff activities are inferred. The internal workbook contains detailed institutional records and is intentionally excluded from the public package. Only aggregate data are shared here, subject to institutional approval.

The source's `50 PoC Initiatives` and `6 Pre-CVP` sheets and `Projects overview` heading provide portfolio context. The latter labels 20 MVP candidates and six pre-commercial value propositions; an additional displayed MVP count is inconsistent with that heading. The management statement reports 500 completed projects, 50 PoCs, 20 MVPs and six finalized CVPs. These counts are provisional and stages may overlap. Do not claim that the register establishes commercial completion or independent acceptance. The 200 hours/user figure lacks a specified accounting interval, denominator and definition of hours. The approximately 100 potential industry links are a pipeline, not a count of confirmed partnerships. The metric above 20,000 has an unspecified unit and cannot be interpreted quantitatively beyond the reported threshold.

## Reviewer interpretation

The dashboard adds institutional context, not independent validation of the university-wide design framework. It does not demonstrate measured throughput, adoption, impact, causal outcomes, or actual benchmark reruns. To substantiate claims, provide a dated deduplicated project register, PoC/MVP/CVP acceptance criteria and records, anonymized aggregate scheduler accounting with counting rules and denominator, and relationship-status records. Independent evaluation requires separate external assessment evidence. Original 35-source and 25-requirement records remain unchanged.
